# DETECTION-OF-PLANT-DISEASES-AND-CONTROL-IN-AGRICULTURE-

## Introduction: 
Being born and brought in the families of farmers watching them working day and night 
with dedication, taking care everyday of something which would be in the end would be a 
product for us; the new modern generation we have been thinking of benefitting their hard 
efforts. The prevention and control of plant diseases have always been widely discussed 
because plants are exposed to outer environment and are highly prone to diseases. 
Normally, the accurate and rapid diagnosis of the plant plays an important role in 
controlling the disease, since useful protection measures are often implemented after 
diagnosis. Hence we have proposed an idea to diagnose the disease of plants using the 
image of infected area and suggest an effective and budget friendly solution for them. We 
will be using image processing algorithms and sorting techniques for the best fitting 
result.

## Implementation: 
The work process starts with the collection of data i.e. the images on the basis of which 
the disease control to be implemented though actual capturing though some crop fields and 
to be specific all the diseased plants and some other datasets. For the filtering procedure 
multiple linear techniques are to be implemented which sorts the images according to the 
species of the plant and their further categorization. After the plants have been grouped 
Naïve Bayes Classification along with clustering using SVMs upon detailed plotting the 
disease, at what rate it is likely to spread under a present area of control, prevention 
methods by suggestion of the type of medicine. With this prototype the detection and 
control is aimed for maximum accuracy and precision which is to be programmed in a way 
that will be a budget friendly alternative as one of its advantage.
